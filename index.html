Terms & Community Rules

Last updated 13 September 2026 · 4 min read

1. Who can use Extroverts

Extroverts is an 18+ platform. By creating an account you confirm that you are at least eighteen (18) years old and legally able to enter into this agreement. Accounts that misstate age are removed without notice and without refund of any Honorary Vibe Tokens.

2. Your account

One human, one account. You are responsible for everything that happens under your handle, including the parties you host and the guests you approve. Keep your login details to yourself; if you believe your account has been accessed by someone else, tell us within 48 hours.

3. Real people, real names

Your display name and profile photo must reasonably represent you. Impersonation, catfishing, or joining a hangout on behalf of somebody else is grounds for immediate removal from the community.

4. Showing up

An RSVP is a promise to a host who has planned around you. Repeated no-shows lower your Vibe Score and may restrict your ability to join exclusive parties. If plans change, cancel in the app so your seat can go to someone on the waitlist.

5. Hosting a party

Hosts set the theme, the guest count, the venue and the time. As a host you are responsible for describing the hangout honestly, for any cost you advertise, and for the safety of the space you have chosen. Extroverts does not organise, supervise or insure events listed on the platform.

6. Safety first

Meet in public the first time. Tell a friend where you are going. Use the in-app share-my-location tool for the duration of a hangout. Never send money to another member outside the platform, and never share a one-time password with anyone our team will never ask for it.

7. Content you post

You keep ownership of the photos, captions and memories you upload. You grant Extroverts a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to store and display that content inside the product so that the people in your hangouts can relive it. You can delete your content at any time.

8. Zero tolerance

Harassment, hate speech, sexual content involving minors, solicitation, drugs, weapons, spam and commercial promotion without a Host Pro licence all result in a permanent ban. Reports are reviewed by a human within 24 hours.

9. Your data

We collect the details you give us during signup (email, name, date of birth, pronouns, city, college and interests) to match you with hangouts that are actually relevant. We do not sell personal data. You may request an export or a full deletion from Settings, Privacy, and we will action it within 30 days.

10. Notifications

By signing up you agree to receive transactional messages about your parties, RSVPs and security. Marketing messages are optional and can be switched off in Settings without affecting your account.

11. Changes to these terms

If we make a material change we will tell you in the app at least seven days before it takes effect. Continuing to use Extroverts after that date means you accept the updated terms.

12. Contact

Questions about these terms? Write to hello@extroverts.app and a human will reply. For grievances under the Information Technology Rules, contact our Grievance Officer at the same address.
